## PAGE: Privacy Policy  (route: `/privacy`)

**Title:** Privacy Policy — Vision9 Solar Inc.

**Effective date:** July 23, 2026

### 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Vision9 Solar Inc. ("Vision9 Solar," "we,"
"us," or "our") accesses, uses, stores, and protects information in connection
with our private business-operations integration with QuickBooks Online (the
"Application"). The Application is an internal tool used solely by Vision9 Solar
and its authorized personnel to manage Vision9 Solar's own business records.

### 2. Scope

This policy applies to data accessed and processed through the Application. It
does not govern any other Vision9 Solar website, product, or service. Because
the Application is a private, internal tool, the "users" of the Application are
Vision9 Solar's own authorized staff, and the data processed is Vision9 Solar's
own business data.

### 3. Information We Access

With authorization granted through Intuit's secure OAuth process, the
Application may access the following categories of data from Vision9 Solar's
QuickBooks Online company:

- Company profile and settings
- Customers and contacts
- Products, services, and items
- Invoices, estimates, sales receipts, and payment status
- Bills, expenses, and purchase orders
- Financial reports (e.g., profit and loss, balance sheet, aging reports)

The Application accesses this data only as needed to perform Vision9 Solar's
bookkeeping, invoicing, reconciliation, and operational tasks. Access is granted
by Vision9 Solar and can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the Application
from within QuickBooks Online.

### 4. How We Use Information

We use the information accessed through the Application to:

- View, create, and maintain Vision9 Solar's accounting and operational records;
- Prepare invoices, estimates, and reports;
- Reconcile records between our operational systems and QuickBooks Online;
- Support AI-assisted review and preparation of the above, subject to human
  review before any change is committed.

We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it.

### 5. AI Processing

The Application uses artificial-intelligence assistance (Anthropic's Claude) to
help prepare and review accounting and operational work. Data sent to this AI
service is processed under the provider's commercial terms and is **not used to
train the provider's AI models**. AI-generated output is reviewed by authorized
Vision9 Solar personnel before any record is created or changed in QuickBooks
Online.

### 6. How We Store and Protect Information

- Data is processed on infrastructure controlled by Vision9 Solar, including an
  on-premise server, rather than on third-party hosted application platforms.
- Access to the Application and its data is restricted to authorized Vision9
  Solar personnel over a private, access-controlled network.
- Authentication credentials and access tokens are stored in protected
  configuration, kept separate from ordinary backups, and encrypted.
- We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards
  appropriate to the sensitivity of the data.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot
guarantee absolute security.

### 7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade information accessed through the Application. We
may disclose information only:

- To service providers (such as our AI processing provider) strictly to operate
  the Application, under confidentiality obligations;
- When required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request;
- To protect the rights, property, or safety of Vision9 Solar, our customers, or
  others.

### 8. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as needed for the operational and record-keeping
purposes described above, or as required by applicable law, after which it is
deleted or de-identified.

### 9. Your Rights

Because the Application processes Vision9 Solar's own business records, requests
regarding that data are handled internally by Vision9 Solar. Individuals with
privacy questions or requests relating to personal information we may hold can
contact us at info@vision9.biz, and we will respond in accordance with
applicable law, including Canada's Personal Information Protection and
Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

### 10. Intuit and QuickBooks Online

The Application connects to QuickBooks Online using Intuit's official APIs and
authorization process. Intuit is not responsible for this Application or its
data practices. Authorization can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the
Application from within QuickBooks Online, after which the Application will no
longer access the connected company's data.

### 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be
reflected by updating the effective date above.
