## Net Metering :

## Residential & Commercial

Net metering is a billing mechanism that allows residential and commercial customers who generate their own electricity from solar panels or other renewable energy sources to offset their consumption and earn credits for excess power they generate. In Ontario, Canada, net metering has become an increasingly popular option for homeowners and businesses who want to reduce their carbon footprint and lower their energy bills.
