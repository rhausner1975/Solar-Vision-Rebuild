Why Most Off-Grid Systems Disappoint

## Off-grid solar fails for predictable reasons.  We've seen all of them.

After a decade maintaining commercial solar installations and building off-grid systems across Ontario,
we've watched the same problems repeat themselves — not because of bad panels, but because of bad mounting
decisions made during installation day.


01

### Fixed-angle mounts lose up to 30% winter output

The sun sits low in winter. A fixed rack built for summer angles sacrifices meaningful production
exactly when you need it most — heating season. Most installers know this and say nothing.

02

### Systems that can't grow without starting over

You buy 4 panels today and 8 more next year. But your existing rack wasn't designed to expand.
Now you're dismantling and rebuilding — at full cost — what should have been a bolt-on expansion.

03

### Installs that need a crew — and still go wrong

Undersized hardware, missing provisions for future cable runs, panels installed at the wrong height
for snow shedding. These are installation-day decisions that cause years of operational headaches.

Panel angles, rail spacing, and mounting height are designed to shed heavy snow loads naturally.
